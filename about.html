Profile

Behind the camera.

I am Muhammad Nabil Firdaus, also known as Nabil Firdaus. I study Communication Science at the University of Mataram and work across photography, videography, visual storytelling and digital content.

My practice spans live performances, event documentation, graduation portraits, café storytelling and commercial visual content. I value thoughtful composition, natural interactions and images that communicate a feeling as much as a moment.

In addition to photography, I have worked in media production, graphic design and social media content strategy.
